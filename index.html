1. Dedicated Window (Desktop)

Modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari) throttle background tabs after ~5 minutes of inactivity. For uninterrupted 60-second checks, pull this tab into its own small browser window or keep it visible on a secondary monitor.

2. Mobile Stay-Awake Mode

On Android/iOS, tap "Enable Push Alerts" and toggle "Screen Stay-Awake: ON" above during major drop windows so your phone doesn't suspend network timers.

3. Audio + OS Push Combo

Keep the audio chime unmuted—even if a window is behind another app, the Web Audio synthesizer chime will play immediately and clicking the OS popup takes you straight to Level Up Store checkout.
